The model

Match rating, every player, every match

From 3.0 to 10.0, starting at 6.0 and moved by what he did, scaled by minutes played:

  • Goals, assists, shots on target, key passes
  • Tackles won, interceptions, saves (keepers), clean sheet share
  • Pass completion against his position's average
  • Minus errors, goals conceded while on the pitch, cards
  • A small share of the result: win, draw or loss

The last 10 ratings are saved per player. Form is their average weighted by minutes, newest heaviest.

A rarely used player who plays well when he does keeps his chemistry: good form fills in part of the missing minutes. A regular who plays badly slowly loses it.

Boost: 1% to 15%, one smooth curve

Applied to the stats the match reads (passing, control, positioning, pressing, first touch). Low chemistry still gives 1%; the curve is steepest in the middle, where most players sit.
