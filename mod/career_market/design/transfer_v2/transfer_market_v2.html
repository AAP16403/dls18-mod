Transfer Market

What replaces what

  • One screen replaces the stock search table, the footer buttons and the separate market pages.
  • Negotiation sheet replaces the stock Sign Player box: fee, contract, the club's replies and a rival's bids in one place.
  • Offers and My Squad replace the Sell Player button and the offer inbox.

What the numbers mean

  • Asking price is the club's quote. Its hidden accept point lies between its minimum and the ask.
  • Patience drains in proportion to how far a bid falls below the club's minimum. Empty means talks end for this window.
  • Chance to join falls smoothly with the step down in club size and team strength.

Try it

  • Open Scout, pick a player, press Make offer and bid low, then higher.
  • Answer the bids in Offers, or list a player in My Squad.
  • Coins, wage room and squad size update after each deal.
